# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient.
- Focus on the work and its impact.
- Give and accept clear, kind feedback.
- Protect private publishing information and credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed and handled fairly and confidentially.
