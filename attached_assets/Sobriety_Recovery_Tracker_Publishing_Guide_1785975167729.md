## 3. Interior Structure

`title_page` → `intro_page` ("How to Use") → `habit_tracker_page(s)` for 90 days → `daily_page` (loop x90) → `milestone_review_page` (at Day 7, 30, 60, 90 instead of plain weekly review — includes a small "Milestone Reached" certificate-style banner) → pad with `blank_notes_page` if under 72-page minimum.

**Daily page fields (replacing ADHD-specific fields):**
- Today's Focus
- Top 3 Priorities
- Time Blocks (6 AM–9 PM, same as base)
- Craving/Urge Level (scale: None / Mild / Moderate / Strong / Intense) — replaces general "Mood"
- Check-in: Meeting attended · Sponsor/support call made · Water (8 cups) · Sleep 7+ hrs
- Gratitude line (1 sentence) — new small addition, common in recovery journaling
- Notes

**Milestone Review page (replaces Weekly Review) — at Days 7/30/60/90:**
- Days sober so far (auto-labeled per milestone)
- What helped me this stretch?
- What was hardest?
- One thing I'm proud of
- Note to future self

All technical margin/bleed/spine rules from the base guideline apply unchanged (0.4in margin, no edge-to-edge banners, spine text only at 79+ pages, 72-page minimum for color).

## 7. Notes Specific to This Topic

- Tone matters more here than in ADHD niche — copy and imagery should feel supportive, never clinical or judgmental. Avoid words like "addict"; use "recovery," "sobriety journey."
- Milestone pages are the emotional core of this book — spend extra design care here, this is the differentiator vs. generic sobriety trackers on Amazon.
- Consider a second version later with a more neutral/masculine palette (many recovery buyers are men) if this one performs well — test palette before committing to a full new SKU.
